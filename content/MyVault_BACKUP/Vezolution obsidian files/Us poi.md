#UNITED_NATIONS 
[[POI's]]
1. **USA, considering your long-standing support for Israel and your repeated vetoes in the UN Security Council against resolutions that would hold Israel accountable for its actions, how do you justify your stance on Palestinian statehood and membership in the United Nations?**
2. **Given the USA's history of promoting democracy and human rights worldwide, how do you reconcile this with your opposition to Palestinian self-determination and statehood, which effectively denies Palestinians the same rights you advocate for elsewhere?**
3. **How does the USA respond to accusations that its financial and military support to Israel perpetuates the occupation and hinders any real progress towards a two-state solution and Palestinian membership in the UN?**

- **Question**: The United States has historically vetoed numerous UN Security Council resolutions critical of Israel’s actions in Palestinian territories. How does this align with the principles of fairness and justice in international law?
    
    - **Possible USA Answer**: The United States believes in Israel’s right to defend itself and seeks balanced resolutions that ensure security and peace for both Israelis and Palestinians. We veto resolutions we view as one-sided or unconstructive.
    - **Retaliation**: While the right to self-defense is recognized, continuous vetoes undermine the UN's ability to hold accountable actions that violate international law. How can the UN function effectively if one member consistently blocks accountability measures?
- **Question**: The US officially recognized Jerusalem as the capital of Israel, a move criticized by many in the international community as undermining the peace process. How does this decision contribute to a viable two-state solution?
    
    - **Possible USA Answer**: The US recognizes Jerusalem as the historical and current capital of Israel but remains committed to facilitating a two-state solution through direct negotiations between the parties involved.
    - **Retaliation**: By taking unilateral actions that pre-empt the outcomes of negotiations, isn't the US compromising its role as an impartial mediator in the peace process?
- **Question**: How does the US justify its substantial financial and military aid to Israel, given the ongoing occupation and alleged human rights violations in Palestinian territories?
    
    - **Possible USA Answer**: US aid to Israel is intended to ensure its security in a volatile region. We also provide humanitarian assistance to Palestinians and support initiatives aimed at improving their economic conditions.
    - **Retaliation**: Given that US aid to Israel far outweighs aid to Palestinians and is used in ways that perpetuate the occupation, how can this be seen as promoting peace and equality?
- **Question**: What is the US stance on Palestinian membership in the United Nations as a full member state, and what conditions does it believe must be met for this to happen?
    
    - **Possible USA Answer**: The US believes that full membership should result from direct negotiations and a mutually agreed-upon peace settlement. We support efforts to build Palestinian institutions and governance structures in the interim.
    - **Retaliation**: Given the prolonged impasse in negotiations and the power imbalance between the parties, isn't it more practical to support UN membership as a way to level the playing field and facilitate a fairer negotiation process?
    
- **Question**: The US often emphasizes its commitment to democracy and human rights worldwide. How does this commitment align with its stance on the Palestinian right to self-determination and statehood?
    
    - **Possible USA Answer**: The US supports the right to self-determination for all peoples, including Palestinians, and believes that a negotiated settlement is the best way to achieve a viable and lasting peace.
    - **Retaliation**: If the US genuinely supports self-determination, shouldn't it advocate for immediate recognition of Palestinian statehood to empower Palestinians in negotiations and help ensure their rights are respected?
