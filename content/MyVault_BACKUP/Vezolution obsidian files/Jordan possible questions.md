#UNITED_NATIONS 
### Questions for Jordan:

1. **Hosting Palestinian Refugees:**
    
    - **Question:** "Jordan, as host to nearly two million Palestinian refugees, how do you address the challenges these refugees face, particularly in terms of legal status, employment opportunities, and access to education and healthcare?"
    - **Possible Retaliation:** "Jordan has made significant efforts to integrate Palestinian refugees into our society, including granting citizenship to many. We provide access to education, healthcare, and employment opportunities, though we acknowledge challenges remain. Our cooperation with international organizations like UNRWA and support from the international community are essential in addressing these issues."
2. **Disparities Between Camp and Non-Camp Populations:**
    
    - **Question:** "Reports indicate stark disparities in human development between camp and non-camp Palestinian populations in Jordan. What specific measures is your government taking to address these inequalities and improve living conditions in the camps?"
    - **Possible Retaliation:** "We are aware of these disparities and are working to improve infrastructure and services in the camps. Initiatives include upgrading housing, improving healthcare facilities, and expanding educational programs. We continue to seek international support to enhance these efforts and ensure equal opportunities for all Palestinian refugees."

1. **Support for Non-Citizen Palestinian Refugees:**
    
    - **Question:** "Non-citizen Palestinian refugees, especially ex-Gazans, face severe restrictions and higher poverty rates. What actions is Jordan taking to mitigate their hardships and improve their living conditions?"
    - **Possible Retaliation:** "Jordan has implemented specific programs to support non-citizen Palestinian refugees, such as providing free government health insurance for children and subsidized healthcare for others. We are also working on infrastructure projects in camps like Jerash to improve living conditions. However, the international community must share the responsibility to ensure comprehensive support."
4. **Economic and Social Integration:**
    
    - **Question:** "How does Jordan plan to further integrate Palestinian refugees into its economic and social fabric, and what role do international partnerships play in this process?"
    - **Possible Retaliation:** "Integration of Palestinian refugees is a priority for Jordan. We focus on vocational training, employment programs, and educational initiatives to facilitate their economic and social inclusion. International partnerships and aid are crucial in supporting these efforts, and we continue to collaborate with organizations like UNRWA to maximize impact."
