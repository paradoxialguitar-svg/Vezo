### Palestine

In 1976, [the US vetoed a resolution](https://unispal.un.org/DPA/DPR/unispal.nsf/0/696D540FD7821BCE0525651C00736250) calling on Israel to withdraw from all Palestinian territory – in this case, the UK, Sweden and Italy abstained.

A draft text presented [by Tunisia in 1980](https://unispal.un.org/DPA/DPR/unispal.nsf/0/E819629C2575BC3E0525652900793664) stressed the “inalienable rights of the Palestinian people”. The US voted against the draft text; the UK, France, Norway and Portugal abstained.

Resolutions condemning Israeli settlements were [blocked only by the US](https://unispal.un.org/DPA/DPR/unispal.nsf/0/45F7F49313F3EA82052565310059C95A) in 1983, [1997](https://unispal.un.org/DPA/DPR/unispal.nsf/0/F97C162F6A30647205256531005B4E15) and [2011](http://www.securitycouncilreport.org/un-documents/document/IP%20S2011%2024.php).

And in 2004 and 2006, the US refused to call on Israel to halt wars against Gaza, which together killed hundreds of civilians.
