#UNITED_NATIONS 
**Discussion Topic: Addressing Gender Discrimination and Violence Against Women in the Occupied Palestinian Territories**

**Introduction:** The situation of Palestinian women and girls in the occupied territories remains a pressing concern. Despite efforts by the Government of Palestine, entrenched discrimination, rights violations, and violence persist due to traditional patriarchal norms and inequitable power dynamics. The fragmentation of Palestinian territory and the inability to convene the Palestinian Legislative Council have hindered significant legislative progress.

**Key Issues for Discussion:**

1. **Entrenched Discrimination and Violence:**
    
    - Despite the implementation of measures such as the second National Action Plan on Women, Peace and Security, violence against women and girls remains prevalent.
    - Discuss the role of societal norms and patriarchal structures in perpetuating gender-based violence and discrimination.
2. **Legislative and Policy Challenges:**
    
    - The State of Palestine’s progress in aligning national legislation with the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women (CEDAW) is stalled.
    - Examine the impact of territorial fragmentation on legislative processes and the functioning of the Palestinian Legislative Council.
3. **Intersectional Experiences:**
    
    - Recognize that Palestinian women are not a homogenous group; their experiences are shaped by socioeconomic status, education level, and residence status.
    - Explore how different identities intersect to create varying experiences of discrimination and access to rights.
4. **Role of Activists and Civil Society:**
    
    - Highlight the crucial role that women activists and civil society organizations play in advocating for the advancement of women's rights.
    - Discuss how these organizations can be supported and their efforts amplified.
5. **Recommendations for Action:**
    
    - Propose a broad range of gender-sensitive legal and institutional reforms to address discrimination and violence.
    - Suggest economic and social measures to enhance the rights and well-being of Palestinian women and girls.

**Discussion Questions:**

1. What specific legislative reforms are needed to better align Palestinian laws with CEDAW?
2. How can the international community support the Palestinian Legislative Council in overcoming the challenges posed by territorial fragmentation?
3. In what ways can civil society organizations be empowered to advocate more effectively for women's rights?
4. What economic and social policies can be implemented to improve the political and economic participation of Palestinian women and girls?
