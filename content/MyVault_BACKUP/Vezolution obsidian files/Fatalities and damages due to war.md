## Great March of Return

Palestinians in Gaza began protesting at the Israeli border fence in March 2018, calling for the “[right of return](https://www.aljazeera.com/opinions/2018/5/15/my-palestinian-right-of-return-is-sacred)” to ancestral homes from which their families were expelled in 1948 during what Palestinians call the “Nakbah”, or the creation of the state of Israel. The UN estimates 750,000 Palestinians were expelled that year.

Palestinians faced [sniper fire from Israeli forces](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2019/3/30/gazas-great-march-of-return-protests-explained) during the year-long protests, which killed at least 266 people and injured roughly 30,000 more, according to Gaza’s health ministry.
